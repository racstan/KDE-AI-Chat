# Pictures used by the demo media

- `lisbon-tram.jpg`: "Tram on Rua Paiva de Andrade, Lisbon Portugal - 2008" by wampile, **public domain**,
  from Wikimedia Commons (https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Tram_on_Rua_Paiva_de_Andrade,_Lisbon_Portugal_-_2008.jpg),
  scaled down to 1200x800. Attached in the video's attachment step.

  "a lighthouse at dusk, watercolor" (768x512, seed 7), with its own mark. Shown as the picture `/image` makes.
